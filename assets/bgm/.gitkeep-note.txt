Place royalty-free / licensed background-music MP3 files in this folder.

- Supported: .mp3 .wav .ogg .m4a
- Do NOT download copyrighted music or use YouTube rips.
- Suggested source: YouTube Audio Library (download manually).
- The pipeline selects a track deterministically per project and loops/trims
  a COPY into projects/<id>/audio/background_music.mp3 (originals untouched).
